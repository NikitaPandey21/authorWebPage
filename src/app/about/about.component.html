About Nikita

Nikita grew up in Pune, India, a city rich in history and culture. She is a software engineer and firmly believes in the power of storytelling to transport readers, challenge perspectives, and evoke deep emotions.

She debuted in the world of literature with the fiction novel The Day I Live For, a story rooted in emotions, introspection, and human connection.

Apart from being a software engineer and an author, Nikita has a deep interest in music and is also a trained classical singer.

Writing, for Nikita, is not just an art but a way to explore unspoken feelings, silent battles, and unconfessed love.
