About Nikita

Nikita Pandey grew up in Pune, India—a city steeped in history, culture, and quiet inspiration. A software engineer by profession, she believes deeply in the power of storytelling to transport readers, challenge perspectives, and stir lasting emotions.

She debuted in the world of literature with the fiction novel The Day I Live For, a story shaped by introspection, emotional depth, and the fragile beauty of human connections.

Alongside writing and technology, Nikita finds solace in music and continues to learn classical singing, drawing emotional nuance from its discipline and depth.

For Nikita, writing is more than an art form—it is a way of exploring unspoken feelings, silent battles, and the kind of love that often remains unconfessed.
